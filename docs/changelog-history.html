Changelog history#

The detailed, stage-by-stage history of the engine up to version 0.1.0, kept for reference. The short release notes are in the changelog. Details of some stages (the development texture pack, scratch measurements) describe private development builds only: released builds ship Pixel Perfection CE as their texture pack.

Development history up to 0.1.0#

Fine water: tall falls and opposing flows (docs/water-fine.md)#

  • Tall falls (fluid.FineRules = 3): a stream covers 256 cells (was 64) and falls with a terminal speed of 30 blocks/s, so a 150-block fall is one stream that lands after 5.5 s instead of three streams restarting from rest in bricks hanging in mid-air (a splash ring and a thin filet in the sky every 64 blocks). The curtain grows with the front of the water (voxel.FineStream.FrontGap/TailGap) instead of appearing whole at the first tick, has no splash before the water arrives, keeps its streaks moving down the whole fall (the fall attribute saturated 64 blocks down), and is meshed in the chunk layers above the terrain that hold no edited block (the curtain had a gap in each). -demo tower -demo-n H in the sandbox.
  • Opposing flows: the water push on bodies (voxel.LiquidTable.Flow) grows with the head difference up to full strength at one level (coarse water is unchanged): a fine film no longer pushes at full speed, and where two flows meet the push no longer flips between full speed one way and the other. Tests for channels fed from both ends, springs facing each other, crossings, loops, a spring against a coarse lake or lava (settling, no oscillation, symmetry, conservation). -demo opposing in the sandbox.

Fine water under overload (water-fine.md, "Overload")#

  • Many water sources no longer freeze the fine water. At FineMaxBricks a refused spill kept its brick dirty, which kept its whole sheet from folding: the pool never emptied, the water stopped half spread with straight cut edges and dry ground between sources, and the world was never idle (100 sources on a plane: 2048 bricks, 1248 of them dirty, forever). The demands were also served from the lowest cell every step: of 300 sources, some never got any water.
  • Rules version 2 (fluid.FineRules = 2; journals recorded under version 1 fail their first hash check, older snapshots restore): demands are served from a hashed spawn cursor in rotation; on a step where the cap refuses water, the calm water behind the waiting fronts folds into coarse cells (overload fold), and when nothing can fold that way, whole components of calm water and waiting fronts fold with the lateral tests waived (forced fold, every 20 steps, at most 1/8 of the cap). Exact conservation at every tick; the scenes settle to the same coarse water as with an unlimited pool.
  • A fold no longer ranks a full brick as a residue (its share did not fit, the cached volume drifted from the fills and a later rollback lost the difference; TestFineFoldResidueFits).
  • FineMaxBricks defaults to 4096 (was 2048). Overload signal: Stats.FineOverload, FineCapRefused, FineCapSources, FineCapSteps, FineOverFolds, FineOverFolded, State.Overloaded/OverloadNote; fluid.Plugin logs entering and leaving an overload (Log, Quiet) and shows the note in the HUD with OverloadHUD (on in the sandbox).
  • Water meshing of large sheets is 4.6x faster (dense brick index, dense memo of the surface candidates and corner normals, same output): an overloaded chunk took up to 140 ms per build, so the drawn water lagged the simulation by seconds; the lateral pass caches the cell under each brick.
  • examples/voxel_sandbox -demo sources -demo-n N -demo-layout plane|terrace|bowl|cliff|cave: the stress scene. Tests: fluid/fine_overload_test.go (settling, fairness, determinism and snapshots, bounded cost with 500 sources, mesh/simulation agreement), BenchmarkFineWaterSheet.

Website, Hub wiring and release pipeline (releasing.md)#

  • cmd/burrowsite + internal/site (make site): a dependency-free, reproducible static site generator for GitHub Pages. Landing page, download page (reads the signed manifest.json, "coming soon" without), the allow-listed docs with sidebar and full-text search, templates and screenshot gallery, changelog and Atom feed, licences and credits, about and brand downloads, 404.html, robots.txt, sitemap.xml, .nojekyll, optional CNAME; English at /, French at /fr/ (docs in English only). Inter bundled, dark and light themes, strict CSP, no external request; screenshots resized and recompressed (38 MiB to about 6 MiB). The build fails on a leak (local paths, private URLs, secrets), a broken link or anchor, an external resource, an unclassified docs page or a size overrun.
  • Hub: DefaultSiteURL (-X burrow-engine/hub.DefaultSiteURL, HUB_SITE_URL in the dist scripts) derives the manifest, docs, news and download addresses; the Home buttons open the website through a same-origin-only opener; the website is editable in developer mode only. Signed, plain-text, size-capped news in the manifest (burrowrelease manifest -news-file, -tag-prefix v); the consent screen names the fetched file and host.
  • release/burrow-release.pub is the committed release public key (the dist scripts default to it); scripts/publish-release.sh (make publish-release) prints the plan by default, builds and signs with --build-only, and publishes to the public releases repository only with --execute and a typed confirmation.

Stabilisation pass#

  • third_party_vulkan: the C copies made for slice arguments (vkCreateGraphicsPipelines, barriers, submits, ...) were freed by a finalizer attached to a dead result variable, i.e. at the next GC, possibly during the call. The finalizer now sits on the allocation map and every wrapper keeps it alive until it returns (tools/fixalloc.pl, run by make regen). Closes the MoltenVK start-up SIGSEGV race.
  • Sound: Pack.Warm decodes the step, block break and other block events first (first variant of every event before the other variants, 96 files); an alphabetical 64 file cap used to leave every step.* event cold, so the first footsteps were dropped.
  • Windows log files are named Burrow.log and BurrowEditor.log; the Windows README describes sounds, diagnostics variables and the F1 help; the FSR and SMAA licences ship in licences\.
  • burrowd exposes 20 tools (test updated).

Scripting completeness#

  • Scene queries: scene_parent, scene_children, scene_path, scene_find_with, scene_find_tag; the tags scene component (at most 16 per node). Results are bounded (4096) and charged gas per node scanned. SDK Parent, Children, ScenePath, FindWith, FindTag, TagsOf, HasTag, AddTag, RemoveTag.
  • Field access: field_get, field_get_num, field_get_many, field_get_nums, field_set, field_set_num, component_patch (RFC 7386 merge patch). Paths such as pos[0] and bodies[2].mass. Partial writes are schema-validated and queued like entity_set_component, so replays are exact. Typed accessors for the built-in components are generated by make sdk-gen (cmd/sdkgen, sdk/burrowsdk/components_gen.go); make sdk-check and a test fail when they are stale.
  • Data: asset_read (project data files, <= 256 KiB, 16 reads per transaction; SDK ReadAsset, ReadAssetJSON; Plugin.ReadAsset hook), block_count, block_name, block_info, registry_list (blocks, components, assets). The engine has no item registry: items are data tables read with ReadAsset.
  • Message bus: bus_subscribe, bus_unsubscribe, bus_subs, bus_publish. Delivered on a later tick in ascending entity order, to on_event with kind BusKind(topic). SDK OnMessage, Publish, Subscribe, Unsubscribe; subscriptions re-established automatically after hot reload.
  • Timers: timer_start, timer_cancel, timer_remaining (simulation time, <= 64 per script). SDK OnTimer, After, Every, CancelTimer, TimerRemaining.
  • Tween and easing (EaseKind, Tween, NewTween, Cooldown): plain data, evaluated at the current tick.
  • Frame look: camera_rig.frame_look (degrees per mouse pixel) lets the view turn at frame rate; the sim still gets the look once per tick. The value must equal the script's sensitivity. camplug feeds it.
  • Dev experience: ScriptFault.Trace and ConsoleEntry.Trace (guest stack, names with burrowscript build -debug), shown in the editor console; Runtime.Profile() (gas, host gas, wall time per script entity); Plugin.PauseOnFault pauses the sim (script.fault).
  • Host function registry: package script/api (api.All, Lookup, Names; Func{Name, Params, Results, Doc, Category, Gas, Free}), built from the ABI and script/ABI.md, for tools and the visual scripting VM.
  • Not bridged: engine trigger and collision events (frame-domain only); use physics_overlap diffing, gameui_poll and level_current polling.
  • Migration: the Script component gained subs and timers; a script that uses neither hashes as before. New scene component tags. Rig field frame_look (default 0). New host functions are ABI additions only; existing names and signatures are unchanged.

Water: evaporation of stagnant films, shallow water at noon (water-fine.md)#

  • Fine water: thin, isolated films (no column deeper than 1/8 block, no coarse water, lava or void beside them, not feeding a stream, a component of at most FineEvapBudget bricks) that stay unchanged for 25 s evaporate little by little (3% of a threshold film per second at noon and 15 C; x1.5 in sun and heat, x0.25 under a roof or underground, slower in the cold, stopped by rain or snow in the open, read from the weather extra when installed). Lakes, rivers, seas, springs and the films touching them never lose water. Every unit goes to Ledger.Evaporated: the conservation law stays exact. New fluid.Config fields FineEvapPeriod (-1 turns it off), FineEvapBudget, FineEvapDepth, FineEvapDwell, FineEvapRate, FineEvapEps; Stats.FineEvapVisited. The dwell of each film and the pass cursor are simulation state (hashed, snapshotted, replayed). On by default with Config.Fine: fine scenes that leave films now end without them (and a thin puddle the evaporation will take stays in bricks instead of folding into level-1 coarse cells; any other thin component folds as before), so their later hashes differ. A fine Config hashes the new rules version fluid.FineRules (1): a journal recorded before fails its first hash check at replay, a snapshot from newer rules is refused, an older save restores. The coarse automaton is unchanged (its unfed level-1 cells already drain). An inert weather module (installed by every game, off unless enabled) does not drive the rate; a slow rate keeps an integer remainder instead of rounding to zero.
  • Fine water mesh: the depth of a top vertex is taken at its corner (continuous across sub-columns) instead of per column, so the depth-driven shading has no stair-stepped squares and a drying film fades without steps.
  • water_hdr.frag: large white blotches on shallow water at noon. The caustics gain is 1 + 1.2 c / (1 + 0.4 c) (it was 1 + 1.4 c, up to x6: a bright bottom clipped and bloom smeared it), the shore and current foam need about 0.3 block of water, the foam rings of a fall's foot fade out over shallow water; NaN guards: pow(x, 2.0) of a negative x in the rings (NaN on NVIDIA, spread by bloom) is a product, the caustic bases are clamped, the normals normalise with a fallback (water2.vert: the spray fade base too). SPIR-V rebuilt; docs/img/shaders-waterfall.png and shaders-filet.png recaptured.

Coloured block light and skylight (lighting.md)#

  • New voxel.LightField: a persistent light field per chunk, block light in 3 colour channels (4 bits each) plus skylight, flood filled through non-opaque and shaped blocks with per-block attenuation (Block.LightLoss; leaves, water that absorbs red first, stained glass that keeps its own colour), incremental add and remove breadth-first searches across chunk borders, chunk load / unload, bounded Step(budget). Settles to the same field in any order (tests: random edits vs a from scratch fill, cross-chunk, load order, drop and reload). Block.EmitColor (additive JSON emit_color), Block.LightLoss (light_loss), more default emitters (froglights, redstone and soul torches, end rod, ...), torch[state] names resolve.
  • Deterministic query for the simulation: ChunkMap.LightAt(reg, p) / voxel.Light (Level(skyDim)), script host import light_at and burrowsdk.LightAt, nav condition dark{max,sky_dim} and View.LightAt, MCP vox_light and editor_light. Derived data: no StateHash change.
  • Streaming: the field runs on its own goroutine (engine/lightrun.go), meshes take padded snapshots (mesh.Opts.Light), chunks are meshed at once with provisional light and re-meshed only when the hash of the light they read changed (border changes reach only the neighbours that read them). Frame time p99 unchanged.
  • Rendering: per-vertex smooth block light + skylight (vertex stays 16 bytes: light bits moved into the high bits of the x / y positions), all shader levels and -look classic: ambient x skylight occlusion, direct sun needs sky, block light 2.4 * l^2.6; caves are dark by day and by night. Soft renderer parity (render.Options.Light, voxel.DemandLight). RT: emitters bounce with their colour.
  • Light debug view (block, sky, level): RenderSettings.LightView, editor View menu, editor_light. Demos -demo lightcave|lightcolors|lavalake. BURROW_LIGHT=off disables the light.
  • Shaders rebuilt; the vertex position high bits must be masked by any shader reading voxel vertices (chunkPos() in common.glsl).

Sound packs#

  • New package audio/soundpack and a user sound pack loader: the game plays your own .ogg / .wav files from a folder, selected by -sounds <dir> (voxel_sandbox, burrowplay), BURROW_SOUNDS, runtime.audio.pack or ~/.burrow/sounds. sounds.json maps events (step.<material>, block.break|place|hit.<material>, player.*, water.*, ambient.*, fluid.*, ui.click, music.*) to weighted variants with volume, pitch range, stream and attenuation; without it a conventional step/ dig/ random/ ambient/ music/ layout is autodetected (audio/soundpack/autodetect_table.go). Hot reload, hostile-input limits, symlinks and path traversal refused, LRU clip cache, streamed music with a random-gap scheduler. New runtime.audio section (pack, fallback, synth, music_*). Footsteps are timed by distance and silent in fly mode; block break / place / hit sounds follow the player's edits. Burrow ships no sound files, the user owns the rights of their files, and a pack is never exported: burrow build and scripts/dist-windows.sh refuse to pack one (audio.md, "Sound packs").
  • Default sound pack: the game binaries now embed assets/sounds/default/ (102 CC0 sound effects from Kenney Impact / RPG / Interface Sounds and six calm music tracks by Juhani Junkala, re-encoded; ~6 MB, +5.7 MB on burrowplay; -tags nosounds removes it), registered by a blank import in burrowplay and voxel_sandbox (the editor does not link it). It is the base layer under a user pack, per event. -sounds off disables every pack. Water, rain, wind, thunder and lava stay silent (no CC0 source yet). New runtime.audio.default_pack lets a project ship a pack it owns through burrow build. Credits in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md and assets/sounds/default/CREDITS.md; the Windows dist copies the licences.
  • The builtin synthesized sounds are now OFF by default (silence for any event without a pack file; fallback defaults to "none"). Opt in with runtime.audio.synth: true, "fallback": "synth", -synth-sounds (voxel_sandbox, burrowplay); they play at half volume. The sandbox, editor and templates are therefore silent until a pack is supplied.

Player movement (creative-mode feel)#

  • app.Controller (used by examples/voxel_sandbox and the burrowplay sandbox through engine): double-tap Space (within Config.DoubleTap, 0.3 s) toggles flight (the F key still does); descending onto the ground exits flight. Flight ignores pitch, Space/E rise and Shift/Q descend, 10.89 blocks/s (x2 sprinting) with momentum (0.18 s to full speed, 0.4 s glide to a stop, vertical alike). Shader levels, stage S1: HDR foundation, atmosphere, exposure and bloom#

  • New -shaders off|low|medium|high|ultra (voxel_sandbox, burrowplay, burrowedit; env BURROW_SHADERS; default medium) and -clouds auto|clear|scattered|broken|overcast|0..1 (env BURROW_CLOUDS). render.ShaderQuality holds the presets. off (and -look classic) is the previous renderer unchanged and stays the soft renderer's reference.
  • HDR frame buffer (RGBA16F, B10G11R11 fallback for the main target) plus an internal render graph (render/vulkan/rgraph.go, post.go): bloom prefilter and mip chain with a soft threshold and Karis weights, eye adaptation (centre-weighted mean log luminance, 1x1 ping-pong history), bloom upsample, and a composite with auto exposure, ACES with highlight roll-off, saturation/contrast, soft grade, vignette and dither. Every post pass has a GPU timestamp (Stats.GPU.Post, printed by -frames as perf: post ms ...).
  • Procedural atmosphere (shaders/atmo.glsl, CPU twin scene/atmo.go): Rayleigh/Mie single scattering with sun transmittance (orange/red low sun, blue hour, afterglow), sun disc and halo, moon disc, stars, drifting clouds (flat layer on low, ray-marched slab with self shadowing on medium+), aerial perspective on terrain and water, overcast dims the sun. The moon is the directional light at night (weak shadows); shadows fade out toward the horizon instead of popping.
  • Known limits: HDR applies to frames without scene components (hybrid voxel+scene frames and offscreen targets keep the off pipeline); no propagated block light yet (emissive texels lean warm and bloom). The make parity targets (view-test) pin -shaders off.

Water and foliage look (natural and vivid looks only; classic is unchanged)#

  • Water (shaders/water.glsl): the flat registry blue is leaned to a blue-green and made translucent with a light-turquoise shallow band that deepens with the liquid column (absorption: red first, then green), richer colour, stronger Fresnel sky reflection, a 1.8x ripple normal with light variation on wave slopes (faded with distance), and thin drifting foam streaks on one-block-deep water instead of the dot lattice. The alpha is capped at 0.88 so deep water is never an opaque flat blue.
  • Foliage (shaders/voxel_body.glsl, soft twin in render/soft/tex.go): leaves are found from the top mip alpha of the tinted layer (< 1 = cut-out texture). They get lifted dark texels (no near-black speckle), a deterministic per-block hue variation (yellow-green to blue-green, render only), softer AO, an ambient floor, light leaking through the canopy (softer sun shadow, wrap term) and no specular/env reflection (leaves were picking up bright sky reflections). Alpha test coverage is kept across mips (alpha scaled by the mip level) so distant leaves do not thin out.
  • Found while debugging: a second hash3 call inside the leaf block made MoltenVK write garbage and blue speckles on the -shaders off natural look; the per-block variation reuses the existing tone hash instead.
  • Known limits: no depth-buffer based shore detection or refraction (the water depth is the mesher's column depth), no real interior leaf lighting (no propagated light), classic look and -shaders off -look classic goldens keep the old water and leaves.
