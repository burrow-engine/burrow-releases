About Burrow

A voxel game engine written in Go. Free to use, MIT licensed, early.

The name

Burrow is a voxel engine written in Go. The engine does not know what game you are making.

The mascot is an animal that lives in a hole, looking out of the ground. The engine sees your world the same way: a grid of blocks you can dig into.

Early release (v0.4). Platform details and known limits: Platforms.

Privacy

This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads no font, script, image or video from another server (it enforces this with a strict Content-Security-Policy). The Burrow Hub contacts the update source only after you agree, downloads one signed file and sends nothing but its version and your operating system in the User-Agent.

Brand

The identity is a cyan mascot peeking out of a voxel burrow of grass and dirt blocks, on deep navy, with a rounded white wordmark. Typeface: Inter (SIL OFL 1.1, bundled on this site). The mascot may be seen as inspired by the Go gopher; the Go gopher was designed by Renée French and is licensed under CC BY 3.0. The mascot is not the official Go gopher.
